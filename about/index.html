About Me

Hello! I'm Nishank Gite, currently pursuing my MASt at the University of Cambridge in Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics. While my core passion lies in Particle Physics, I've also developed a deep interest in Machine Learning and AI. This has led me to focus on applications like jet tagging in high-energy physics. Beyond the intersection of these fields, I’m excited about leveraging machine learning to solve real-world problems, from optimizing financial markets to improving everyday tasks and beyond.

I’ve had the privilege to work on various exciting projects, including optimizing jet clustering algorithms, exploring AI-driven solutions in my startup CulinAIry Inc., and conducting advanced research with the ATLAS Collaboration. My journey blends both academic rigor and real-world innovation.
